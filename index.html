A little about me

One person, a handful of callings.

I served in the U.S. Navy, and the habits it gave me — show up, do the work, take care of your people — never really left. Today I live in Middle Tennessee with my family, and I spend my days building things that give people back their most limited resource: time.

That takes a few shapes. Through Volunteer AI I help local small businesses actually put artificial intelligence to work — starting with an AI receptionist that answers every call. Through Found by Ford my wife and I turn estate-sale finds into curated vintage treasures. And with Skorrd I build the software I wished I had as a reseller.

I'm one person doing a lot of it — and I like it that way. It keeps me close to the work, close to the people I serve, and honest about what actually matters.

What grounds me

Faith & family come first.

Everything else is built around them. When I'm not working, you'll find me at church or with the people I love most.

My faith & my church

I serve as a deacon at The Glade Church and teach the middle-school class — which, if you've ever taught middle schoolers, you know keeps a man humble and on his toes. Serving here is the most important work I do all week.

My family

My family is the heart of it all — my wife, my kids, and my grandkids. When the work's done, you'll most likely find me on the water with a line in, thanking God for a good day and good people to share it with.
